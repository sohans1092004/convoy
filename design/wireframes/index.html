Convoy · P0 wireframes

Grayscale, static HTML, one shared stylesheet, no JavaScript. Each screen has a state switcher (all 8 states from PLAN §5.2) and a width switcher (360 px mobile / desktop). Everything works by keyboard: Tab to a group, then use the arrow keys.

  • 1 · Discover and compare
    Model cards, variant comparison, on-road estimates with source dates, saved cars, mobile filters
  • 2 · Join a pool
    Minimum, interested vs committed, accepted variants, deadline, deposit terms and pre-commit cancellation copy, all before the Commit button
  • 3 · Compare offers
    Dealer cards, itemised breakdown, evidence per line, tier ladder ("price can only fall"), stock, delivery
  • 4 · Accept and confirm
    Ceiling price, deposit status, live tier status, deadline, cancellation terms, explicit pending/failed states
  • 5 · Track the order
    Timeline, QR voucher, dealer confirmation, delivery date, support actions, invoice confirmation
  • 6 · Dealer quote builder
    Live engine summary, validation, tier editor with what-if preview, confirmation dialog, versions, rank with the published rule
  • 7 · Order timeline with a dispute opened
    Delivery overdue → support case under review, with evidence and history

Content is realistic: catalogue prices come from official pages (24 Oct 2026) and on-road figures from the reference price engine. Dealers are synthetic. Times are in IST. Every payment screen carries "TEST MODE: no real money".
